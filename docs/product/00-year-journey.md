# Your Year of Quest

The Quest has a lot in it, and none of it needs to arrive at once. This chapter is a road for the school year: what to bring into your classes, in what order, and where in this guidebook to read about each step. It is a suggestion, not a rule. Go faster when a class is ready, slower when it is not, and leave out what does not suit your room.

## Why one step at a time

- **The core carries the first weeks.** Stars for the four virtues, attendance, the map and the crown at the end of the lesson are enough for a whole month. Children need time to understand that a star always comes from something you saw.
- **One new layer every two to four weeks.** Each new piece has time to become a habit before the next one arrives, and the class has something fresh to look forward to all year.
- **Introduce it in front of the class.** Open the new screen on the projector, explain it in two sentences, and use it straight away. Five minutes is enough.
- **Your lesson comes first.** If something slows the lesson down, park it and come back to it later in the year.

## What arrives by itself

Some moments come on their own, on dates the Quest already knows. You do not switch them on; it helps to know they are coming. The Ceremony of the Month appears early every month, the Realm Raids arrive three times a year, and the Festival Stalls open around the holidays.

## When it happens, read this

Other moments come when a class or a child gets there, whatever the month. When one of these appears for the first time, it is a good moment to read its section.

## If you started later

Many schools start the Quest after September, and a class can join in the middle of the year. Choose the month you started in at the top of the road: the stops move with it. With fewer months left, put two small stops in one month (for example Hero's Boon with the Market), and leave the rest for next school year. The Ceremony of the Month, the Realm Raids and the Grand Guild Ceremony keep their own dates whenever you start.

## Younger classes

Pre-Junior classes follow the same road at a gentler pace. Their Ceremony of the Month is the Growth Festival garden, with no ranks, and the public screens never show their stars or marks. Keep each new step very short, with lots of pictures and few words, and leave the Training Grounds and the Quest Remote for when the routines are easy.

## How this feeds the rest of the Quest

- Every stop links to the chapter that explains it in full.
- The **Adventurer's Guide** (the **i** in the header) has the same features in short, on the classroom screen.
- The road is the same on every plan; steps that are not on your plan are marked, so you can skip them.
