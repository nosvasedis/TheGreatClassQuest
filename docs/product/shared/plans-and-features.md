# Plans and features

The school’s plan is **Starter**, **Pro**, or **Elite**. The limits below are the usual ones. Your school’s plan may be set up slightly differently, so if a button is locked in the app, the upgrade message on it tells you what your plan includes.

## At a glance

Typical limits: **Starter** 3 teachers / 6 classes. **Pro** 6 teachers / 10 classes. **Elite** unlimited.

**On every plan:** Award Stars, Team Quest, Hero’s Challenge, Ceremony of the Month, Quest Assignment and attendance tools, bounties, Mystic Market artifacts, Hero’s Boon, Teacher Boon, Projector Mode, the Hero’s Chronicle with the Oracle’s reading of your notes, and the Class Greenhouse (its AI pages, the Gardener’s Almanac and the Oracle’s counsels, are Elite).

**From Pro:** Guild Hall (Wheel, Ledger, sorting quiz), Hero Path, Quest Calendar / Day Planner (including all five Special Quests), Scholar’s Scroll and Starfall / make-ups, full Adventure Log (manual diary, Hero of the Day, Hall of Heroes), Hero Campfire and Ember Oaths, Quest Remote (the phone as a Wand for the projector, Showdown Arena), Attendance Chronicle, Family Access, My Planning (class end dates), class grading override.

**From Elite:** Training Grounds (Story Weavers and three more class games), Familiars, Quiz of the Week, School Office, and AI (chronicler, images, Avatar Forge, Oracle, Restock, nameday, certificates and reports).

## Prices and billing

Prices are per school, before VAT (Greek VAT, 24%, is added at checkout).

| Plan | Monthly (September to June) | Yearly (the school year) |
|------|------|------|
| **Starter** | €19 | €150 |
| **Pro** | €35 | €280 |
| **Elite** | €49 | €390 |

- **Monthly** is charged on the 1st, **September to June only**: ten payments. July and August are free and the school keeps its access all summer.
- **Yearly** covers the school year to 31 August and renews every 1 September. It costs about a fifth less than ten monthly payments. A school that joins mid-year pays only for the rest of that year.
- A school that has not chosen a paid plan (or whose plan has ended) is **locked** until it picks one. There is no grace period. The teacher sees a plan screen with the three plans; families see only a short message that the school is paused, never prices.
- The Secretary can open **View plan and billing** in Admin. A school that pays online can change plan or update its card there.

## Each plan in one sentence

- **Starter:** You can run a beautiful star classroom, a monthly map race, a student race, a shop of power-ups, and Projector Mode.  
- **Pro:** You also get houses, a calendar that respects holidays, academic scroll, a diary with Hero of the Day, Hero Campfire and Ember Oaths, Quest Remote (your phone runs the projector), parent logins, and Hero Path.  
- **Elite:** The Quest writes with you — stories, pictures, familiars, weekly quiz, and a School Office.

## If you are unsure

Check this table before you tell a colleague or a parent what a plan includes. Familiars are not on Pro, and the School Office is not on Starter.
