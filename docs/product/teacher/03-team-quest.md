# Team Quest

Team Quest is **class versus class**, inside the same **Quest League**. Sections of the same year (for example two Junior B groups) race each month to finish the map first. The winning class is celebrated in the first half of the **Ceremony of the Month**.

## Purpose

Build **social cohesion**. Nobody is left as a lonely high-scorer: every star a child earns also moves the *class token* along the road. The map makes “we are doing well” visible to a seven-year-old.

## What you see

- A **league banner** and a picker if you teach more than one league.
- The **League Map**: a living, painted world with one road through four realms (the same map the class sees). It opens like a real treasure map: the wax seal cracks, the scroll unrolls as an ink sketch, and the colours wash in. Then it moves gently: the great waterfall pours, water sparkles, chimneys smoke and the citadel fountain plays, birds cross the sky, lights run up the floating stair, the isles of the Crystal Realm drift and the Portal shimmers.
- **The map's sky is the real sky.** It shares the app's live weather and sun times (the same sky as the header and the Home weather card): the sun walks across at the real time, the moon shows tonight's real phase, and clouds, rain, snow, fog, thunderstorms (with lightning), rainbows after showers and the wind all follow the weather outside. Dawn, golden hour, twilight and night light the land; at night every window and lantern glows and fireflies come out. The land itself is painted in the season: blossom in spring, deep green in summer, gold and russet in autumn, snow over the whole land in winter.
- **The places on the map are alive.** Point at the village and its windmill (Bronze Meadows), the mountain gate (Silver Peaks), the Golden Citadel, the floating isles (Crystal Realm) or the Portal: it glows and a small card says which parties are there now, how far the leading party still is from a realm still hidden in the clouds, or that every party has passed through. Click a place to open its Realm Overview, or the Portal to open the Crystal Portal. A touch anywhere on the land leaves a little sparkle of magic.
- **The road remembers the leaders.** A realm no class has reached yet hides under clouds; when the first class gets there, the clouds part and the realm celebrates. Lanterns along the road are lit as far as the furthest class has walked, the leading class flies its banner over the furthest realm reached, and when a class reaches the Portal a pillar of light rises from it.
- **Explore the road.** Point at a class to light up its whole journey: the rest of the map steps into shade and a flag marks its next stop with the stars still to go. Point anywhere on the road and a pin says how far along it is, which realm it is in, and how many stars your selected class (or the leader) still needs to stand there. Crests on the road mark where Silver Peaks, the Golden Citadel and the Crystal Realm begin; they light up once a class reaches them. Faint lights run up the explored road to the leading class, footsteps glow where classes walk, and on a strong computer a warm lantern light follows the mouse.
- **Zoom and look around.** Double-click the land, use the **+** and **−** buttons, pinch on a touch screen or hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom; drag to look around; the **⤢** button (or the 0 key) shows the whole map again. Click a road crest to fly to that gate.
- **Switching classes.** Picking the general view in the header rolls the map back up and seals it until you choose a class or a league again; choosing a class in another league rolls up the old map and unrolls the new one.
- On a slower computer the map turns off the small extras by itself (creatures, the tilt and the mouse depth), and the world, the road and the classes stay. With **reduced motion** turned on in the computer’s settings, nothing moves.
- **Find on map** (from Quest Chronicles) glides the view toward that class and opens its card.
- Each class as a moving token. Position is **progress toward this month’s goal**, not raw star count alone. Labels are nudged so overlapping classes stay readable.
- A list of classes with stars, goal, **quest difficulty level**, and zone.
- **History** opens the **League Archive**, an atlas of past months. It opens on the latest finished month, in your league (or **All leagues**). Step through the months with the arrows or tap one on the dotted route; switch league with the chips, or **Find a class** by name. **Your parties** shows where each of your classes finished. Each league’s page shows the **Month champion**, a **race chart** of where every class stopped on the road (the realms and the goal flag), and then every class with its rank, % of goal, stars against goal, the realm it reached (or the stars it was short), the stars it needed to catch the class ahead, its difficulty level, “Goal reached” and the lesson days lost. Classes with no stars that month wait under **Still at the start line**. Only this school year’s finished months are charted.
- A reminder on **Home** when last month’s **Ceremony of the Month** has not been run yet; the ceremony opens from there.

Only classes that share the same **Quest League** race each other. Junior B does not race Class C.

### Quest Leagues (who races whom)

The league is the class’s age / curriculum band, set when you create the class. It is **not** a guild and **not** a Hero Path class.

Pre-Junior (5–7) · Junior A (7–8) · Junior B (8–9) · A (9–10) · B (10–11) · C (11–12) · D (12–13).

A class’s progress is its stars this month (including Team Quest bonuses such as Pathfinder’s Map or Quiz bonuses) divided by this month’s goal. The four lands sit on the map above, as they do in the classroom: **Bronze Meadows** from 0%, **Silver Peaks** from 30%, **Golden Citadel** from 60%, **Crystal Realm** from 85%.

Places are never shared: if two classes sit on the same percentage, the app still gives each one its own place.

## Quest difficulty levels

Every class has a **quest level** that starts at **Level 1**. Each time the class **reaches this month’s goal**, its level goes up by one, so later months ask a little more. The level shows as a badge on the tab and as a chip in the ceremony (Levels 1–6: 🌱 💧 🛡️ 🔮 🔥 🐉; the display stops at 6). Past months in History and the ceremony show the level that was true *that* month, not today’s harder one.

The level has nothing to do with Quest League: two Junior B classes can sit at different levels if one finished the map more often.

## How the monthly goal is set

You do not type the goal. The Quest works it out, so that a class with many holidays is not punished and a class that has already conquered the map is not racing an easy target.

For each student the goal starts at **18** stars, plus **2.5** more for every level above Level 1. That figure is then adjusted for holidays (see below), multiplied by the number of students, and rounded. The class goal **never falls below 18** stars in total (even an empty class is treated as 18).

### Holidays and cancelled lessons

**Days lost** this month are counted from two things:

1. The days of this month that fall inside a **school-wide holiday**, which the School Office sets under School Details; **plus**
2. This class’s **cancelled lessons** that month that are *not* already inside a holiday (cancelled in the Day Planner).

**How much of the month counts**

- **June** always counts as half a month (**0.5**), because term ends. This is not optional.
- Any other month: the days left after the days lost, divided by the days in the month, but never below 60% and never above 100%. A class cannot be asked for a full goal after a long Easter break, and a short holiday cannot shrink the goal below 60%.

Home and Team Quest both show a small calendar or umbrella cue when the goal has been adjusted for days lost.

A worked example (not a promise of any particular class’s number): 10 students at Level 1 in a full month have a goal of about **180** stars. The same class at Level 3 (two levels up) has about **230**. In June those figures are halved, and never go below 18.

## What you can do

- Watch the race with the class on the projector (or on this tab).
- Open **History** to recall last month’s finishing order.
- Run the **Ceremony of the Month** when it is offered — that ceremony *includes* Team Quest ranks and then switches to Prodigy (see the ceremony chapter).
- Select a class in the header so the map highlights the group in front of you.

You do not “award Team Quest points” by hand. Award Stars, Pathfinder’s Map, Quiz of the Week, and some Fortune’s Wheel and event bonuses add the numbers for you.

## How this feeds the rest of the Quest

| Input | Effect on Team Quest |
|-------|----------------------|
| Ordinary stars on Award Stars | Monthly stars for every student, summed for the class |
| Pathfinder’s Map | Instant **+10** class quest stars, once per class per month |
| Quiz of the Week | Extra class quest bonus by performance tier |
| 2x Star Day / Reason Bonus Day | More stars that month, so the token moves faster |
| Holidays / cancelled lessons | Lower goal, fairer race |
| Realm Raid victory | **+5%** of the class's own monthly goal (another **+2.5%** for a Legendary victory) |

Team Quest **resets each calendar month**. Guild Hall does **not**. Do not mix the two races in a parent meeting.

## Realm Raid: the whole school on one side

Three times a year the whole school stops racing and fights together. **Eldhorn**, a great crystal-antlered stag who guards the Realm, comes for one week in a festival coat:

| Raid | When |
|------|------|
| **The Winter Raid** | The 7 days before the Christmas holiday starts (from the school's holiday list; 16 to 22 December if none is saved) |
| **The Carnival Raid** | The 7 days before Clean Monday |
| **The Summer Raid** | The last school week: the 7 days before the school year closes, never later than 14 June |

A week before, a **Realm Raid** pill appears on Home ("Eldhorn arrives in 5 days"). It stays for ten days after the raid with the result. On the projector the remote gets a shield button, and the deck has a Realm Raid card.

**The shield is fair by design.** It is made of one **shard** per class. A shard's size is what that class's own Team Quest map expects from it in its raid lessons, at 80%: heroes × raid lessons × the class's normal stars per lesson. A class of 8 that meets once has a small shard; a class of 16 that meets twice has a big one. The same normal, good week breaks either of them. Leagues don't change it, and a cancelled lesson or a holiday shrinks the shard.

**Every star counts.** Every star any class earns that week sends light into its shard. Stars past a class's shard keep helping the school. When all the stars together fill the whole shield, it breaks for everyone, and the victory moment plays on whichever screen is open (once per device).

| Spoils | Who | What |
|--------|-----|------|
| **Victory** (the shield breaks) | Every class | **+5 Gold** for every hero, a **5% step** on its Team Quest map, and one **festival treasure** for its **Raid Hero** |
| **Legendary** (120% of the shield) | Every class | **+3 Gold** more for every hero and another **2.5% step** |
| **Valor** (a class breaks its own shard) | That class | **+2 Gold** for every hero, even if the shield holds |

The **Raid Hero** is the child who struck the shield in the most of the class's raid lessons, then the most stars, then the fewest Raid Hero prizes before. Open the Raid Hall in the class's last raid lesson and press **Reveal our Raid Hero**. The treasure comes from the raid's own festival stall (Christmas, Carnival or the End of Year Fair) when it has stock, otherwise from this month's stall, and is taken out of stock like a purchase (10 Gold if both are empty). If nobody reveals it, the app picks the hero quietly three days after the raid.

Each teacher's app pays its own classes, once, and you get the usual notification when it does. **Nothing is ever taken away**: if the shield holds, Eldhorn bows and comes back next term.

Tap the pill to open the **Raid Hall**: Eldhorn and the shield, the stars so far, your class's shard, the spoils, and **The Alliance**, a banner for every class. Tap a banner to feature that class. While the hall is open, new stars fly from a class's banner into its shard.
