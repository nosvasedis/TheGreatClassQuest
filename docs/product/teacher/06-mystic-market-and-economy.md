# Mystic Market and the Gold economy

Mystic Market is its **own tab** — not a corner of Hero’s Challenge. Choose a shopper, see their Gold, and buy from four shelves: **Legendary Artifacts** (always the same), **Festival Stall** (timed holidays), **seasonal treasures** (monthly classroom flavour), and **Familiar eggs** (Elite).

## Purpose

Teach **delayed gratification**. Stars become purchasing power. Rank on Hero’s Challenge is **not** for sale: spending Gold never lowers Total or Monthly stars.

## How Gold is earned

Every star you award also gives **1 Gold**. Gold is kept separate from rank.

Extra Gold can arrive from:

| Source | What happens |
|--------|----------------|
| **2× Star Day** | Stars (and therefore Gold) double that day |
| **Hero Path match (Pro)** | **+10 Gold** when the award reason matches the student’s Hero Class |
| **Skill Tree perks (Pro)** | Extra Gold to self, classmates, or guildmates on matching reasons; some first-of-month guild gifts |
| **Scroll of the Gilded Star** | Next star pays **triple Gold** |
| **Fortune’s Wheel** | Gold showers, rushes, and similar segments |
| **Birthday / nameday** | Stars (and Gold) on the special occasion |
| **Quiz of the Week** | 2 Gold per first-try answer, 1 per rescue (at most 6), +1 Gold for a brave try; the Quiz Champion takes one treasure from the stall (it leaves the stock) |
| **Coin Purse** (Teacher Settings) | Fixing a balance by hand |

Do not hand out Gold instead of stars. The star is the act of recognition; Gold simply follows it.

## How Gold is spent

- Market purchases (artifacts, seasonal items, eggs)
- **Hero’s Boon** (15 Gold unless Compassion Token has made boons free this month)

## Seasonal treasures (Elite)

The monthly stall follows **this month’s classroom story** (September is harvest and back-to-school, not Halloween) and is written for the league’s age: Pre-Junior gets cosy toys and six-word descriptions with sticker pictures, while C and D get crafted artefacts and nothing babyish. Each stall is one of the month’s three **collections** (October: Mushroom Ring, Chestnut Fire, Foggy Forest Library), shown next to the month on the shelf. Elite fills **15** kinds automatically when the stall is empty — keep teaching; pictures land in the background. Tap the round brass **Restock** button (circling arrows, at the left end of the counter; it reads "Restock the shelves" when you point at it) for a brand-new monthly stall from a different collection; today’s treasures stay until the new pictures are ready. Festival Stall is separate and arrives on its own.

**Smart stock.** Copies follow the size of the league that shares your stall (your classes in that league): about one common for every two children, rare and legendary pieces much scarcer. For 24 children that is 3 copies of each common, 1 of each rare and 1 of each legendary; 10 children get 2, 1 and 1; 36 children get 4, 2 and 1. Copies already sold stay sold when a class grows. Buying spends one copy; when the last copy goes, the treasure is **sold out for the month**. The stall does not refill it.

**Fair shares.**

- One of each treasure per hero.
- Per hero per stall: up to 5 common, 2 rare and 1 legendary (Festival Stall: 2 common, 1 rare, 1 legendary).
- With two or more classes in a league, each class may take its share of each rarity, sized by how many children it has (always at least one). In the **last 7 days of the month** (the last 3 days of a festival) anything left is open to every class.

**How treasures work.** The **How treasures work** button on the Seasonal Treasures sign opens a one-page guide over the Market: the price of each rarity, the charms each rarity can carry, the Hoard ranks, and this league's fair shares in live numbers. With a shopper chosen at the till, it also shows that hero's own Hoard and how far they are from the next rank. It is a good page to put on the projector the first time the class shops.

## What treasures do: Charms

Every seasonal and festival treasure carries a **Charm**: one small classroom privilege, shown on its card in the Market. Common charms are quick (Seat of Choice, Teacher's Helper, Game Chooser, Golden Pen, First Turn, Name Picker). Rare charms are stronger (Partner Pick, Wise Owl's Hint, Music Keeper, Team Captain, Spotlight Share). Legendary charms are the big ones (Teacher for Five, Royal Decree, Teacher's Desk, Wall of Fame). Charms are small lesson moments only: they never touch stars, Glory, Gold, marks, homework or tests. Younger leagues read simpler wording.

Use a charm from the **Trophy Room** (Charms section). The class sees it in the app's normal notification, and you honour it in the lesson. The treasure stays in the collection; only the charm is spent. One charm per hero per day, and up to 3 per class per day. Treasures bought before charms existed carry a common charm.

## What treasures count for: the Collector's Hoard

Every treasure (bought or won as a quiz or raid prize) adds Hoard points: common 1, rare 3, legendary 6, plus 1 for a festival piece. New Hoard ranks pay a little Gold once: Trinket Finder (3 points, +3), Treasure Seeker (8, +5), Curator (15, +8), Hoard Keeper (25, +12), Legendary Collector (40, +15), Keeper of Wonders (60, +20). Rank-ups are paid on the hero's next purchase. The Hoard shows in the Trophy Room. Charms and the Hoard never touch stars, Glory, Team Quest goals or any ranking.

- 5 Common kinds (about 10–18 Gold)
- 5 Rare kinds (about 35–50)
- 5 Legendary seasonal trophies (about 80–120)

Junior language and art stay toy-like; senior items read as RPG relics.

## Festival Stall (Elite)

A small extra shelf for **Halloween** (31 Oct), **Christmas** (25 Dec), **New Year’s Luck** (2 to 25 Jan, the vasilopita season), Greek **Carnival / Apokries**, **Orthodox Easter**, **May Day Flowers** (two weeks before 1 May) and the **End of Year Fair** (all of June). Halloween, Christmas, Apokries and Easter appear about **22 days** before the feast, once their stock is ready; each stall is removed the day after its celebration. When Easter falls late, its stall takes the place of May Day. The Market shows a banner (and a one-time toast) when limited festival treasures have arrived.

Festival kinds: 2 Common, 2 Rare, 1 Legendary, sized to the league the same way. A sold-out festival treasure stays sold out until the festival ends. School Office holiday ranges do **not** drive this shelf.

### Shop discounts (seasonal shelf)

These add together, up to a limit:

- **Reigning Hero of the Day:** **25%** off
- This school year's Hero of the Day wins: Rising Legend (3+) **+5%**, Golden (5+) **+10%**, Mythic (10+) **+15%**. A new year starts at Future Legend. Last year's crowns stay in last year's Hall of Heroes.
- Combined discount **cannot exceed 40%**
- **Aurum Satchel** then halves the resulting price (minimum 1 Gold)

Legendary Artifacts on the evergreen shelf follow their own two-per-month rule; Hero discounts are for the **seasonal** price tag.

## Familiars (Elite)

Each student has **one** Familiar, a companion for the whole year.

| Egg | Gold | Personality | Forms (hatch → mid → late) |
|-----|------|-------------|----------------------------|
| **Thornback** | 30 | Sturdy, loyal | Moss Toad → Bark Bear → Ancient Treant |
| **Frostpaw** | 35 | Calm, wise | Ice Cub → Snow Fox → Frost Guardian |
| **Emberfang** | 40 | Bold, fierce | Hatchling → Flame Drake → Inferno Dragon |
| **Veilshade** | 45 | Mysterious | Shadow Wisp → Phantom Cat → Void Stalker |
| **Sparkling** | 50 | Bright, joyful | Sun Sprite → Dawn Fairy → Solar Phoenix |

**Growth** (stars earned *after* the relevant moment):

1. Egg: after **20** stars earned since you bought it, it hatches (Level 1) and gets a name and an animated picture
2. **60** stars since hatch → Level 2
3. **140** stars since hatch → Level 3

A Familiar sits beside its student’s portrait on the boards, and larger when you open its stats (“stars together” and progress to the next form). Hatching and evolving have their own sounds. On Elite, AI draws each Familiar’s pictures.

## What you can do in the tab

1. Select the class (header) and the **shopper**. The shopper picker and the **Purse** sit side by side as wooden plaques on the counter (on a phone the shopper takes its own row, with the purse below). The **Market Keeper** (the shopkeeper at the counter) greets them, reads out their Gold, and says how many wares they can afford. Point at a ware and he names its price, or how much more Gold the child needs.
2. Browse the shelves; explain prices out loud — children should *choose*. **Aisle signs** jump to one department (Festival Stall, Seasonal, Artifacts, Familiar Eggs), **Sort** orders every shelf by cheapest, priciest, or A to Z (remembered on this device), and **Can afford** hides whatever the shopper cannot buy right now.
3. Each ware shows a paper price tag. With a shopper chosen, the button reads **Buy for N**, **Need N more**, **Owned**, or the limit that blocks it. Discounts (Hero of the Day, Hero-of-the-Day legend tiers, Aurum Satchel) put a sale burst with the percentage on the ware and strike through the old price.
4. **Buy**. Coins hop out of the purse and the Keeper thanks the shopper; when the last copy of a seasonal or festival piece sells, the ware is stamped **Sold!** and leaves the shelf. Then comes **the Keeper’s Parcel**: a wrapped parcel lands on the counter, the wax seal pops, the lid flies off and the ware rises into the lantern light. The Keeper’s receipt tears out with the price (discounts struck through), the purse ticking down and a **PAID** stamp, plus a line from the Keeper. Legendary artifacts arrive in a violet-and-gold parcel, Familiar eggs in a woven basket, and the last copy of a ware says so. Tap the card to skip the unwrapping; **Esc** or a click outside closes it, and it closes by itself after a few seconds unless you hover over it. With **reduced motion** turned on you see the finished scene at once. Inventory appears in Trophy Room.
5. Elite: the brass **Restock** button on the counter brings a brand-new monthly stall (it spins while the new stall is being made). Festival Stall arrives and leaves on its own. **Teacher Settings → Market** repairs a black picture, remaining copies, names, Gold, or descriptions, or replaces one piece.
6. If no class is selected, the shopfront stays curtained with a **Closed** sign (and the sign over the door says Closed too).

## How this feeds the rest of the Quest

Artifacts change **lessons** (luck, bounties, logs, Starfall, Story Weavers), **Team Quest** (Pathfinder), and **Guild Hall** (small Glory gifts, a Guild Standard on the banner, a gilded Wheel). None of them multiplies Glory, so Gold can never buy a Chapter. Familiars make every future star visible. Compassion Token makes Hero’s Boons free for a month.
