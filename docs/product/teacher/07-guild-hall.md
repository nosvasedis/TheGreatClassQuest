# Guild Hall

Guild Hall is **guild versus guild** for the **whole school year**. Students from every level join **one house** and feed that house whenever they earn stars. In June, one guild is crowned at the **Grand Guild Ceremony**.

This is the third competition pillar. It does **not** reset in January. Team Quest does.

## Purpose

**Belonging across ages.** A quiet Junior and a bold Senior can share a motto. Scoring is **Guild Power**, built so a small house is not doomed by headcount.

## The four guilds

Four houses. Four temperaments. The emblems, anthems, and colours are the same everywhere in the Quest — on clouds, on the roster, in the Hall.

**Dragon Flame** — *Fear nothing. Burn bright.* Courage, Fire, Bold. The house of first steps and loud hearts. Children who volunteer, who try the hard sentence, who lead a pair.

**Grizzly Might** — *Stand together. Stand strong.* Strength, Teamwork, Steadfast. The house of the circle: they wait for a classmate, they share the map, they do not leave anyone behind.

**Owl Wisdom** — *Knowledge is power.* Wisdom, Curiosity, Calm. The house of the question. They notice the pattern, they ask *why*, they keep the room thinking.

**Phoenix Rising** — *Fall down seven, rise up eight.* Resilience, Renewal, Hope. The house of the second chance. Welcome Back belongs in their story. They come back to the work.

Each has an emblem, a sound, and a **karaoke anthem**. Tap the emblem in Guild Hall for **lore** (motto, traits, Power, Glory, stars). Play the anthem when the room needs identity, not noise for its own sake.

## Guild Placement Quiz

From **Teacher Settings → My Classes → Manage Students**, any student without a guild can take a **7-question**, story-style quiz. The hat on the roster opens it.

The question pool **matches the league** — Nursery language is not Proficiency language. Weighted answers assign a house. Treat it as identity, not a test. Once placed, the house is for the year (and the design is lifelong belonging).

Starter schools see a locked hat. **Pro** and **Elite** run the quiz.

## Glory and Guild Power

Every positive star writes **Guild Glory** at **2 Glory per star**, plus extras from Wheel, Quiz, and Glory artifacts.

**Guild Power** (what the crystal columns rank) is a fair mix. Each piece is scored **0–100 against the leading house**, then weighted. Glory is always **per member** first — a house of eight who all play can beat a house of twenty where four children carry the crystal.

| Weight | Signal | What it actually measures |
|--------|--------|---------------------------|
| **70%** | Season Glory per member | All Glory this school year, divided by how many students are in the house, then compared with the house that is richest per member. This is the long story. |
| **15%** | This week’s Glory per member | The same idea for **Monday–Friday this week**. A house that is earning *now* can move without having stacked Glory since September. |
| **10%** | Activity | Share of members who **earned Glory this week**. Twenty names on the roll with four active children scores lower than eight names who all showed up. |
| **5%** | Momentum | This week’s Glory versus **last week’s**. A rise gives a small lift; a drop hurts a little (a flat week sits in the middle). **Momentum Lock** from Fortune’s Wheel can stop the score from falling. |

Tap the Guild Power hint in the Hall to see this mix on screen. A busy small guild can beat a sleepy large one. That is the pedagogical point — say it aloud.

The Hall stays **frozen** until the school year has begun and schedules exist. Do not panic on setup week.

## What you see on the tab

- Four **crystal columns**, ranked by Guild Power, with fill, rank-change arrows, member counts
- **This Month’s Champion** per guild (top earner; also badged on Hero’s Challenge)
- Active **Wheel boon chips** on columns
- **Magical Analytics** when you expand a column: champions, top heroes, class mix, contribution, activity
- **Fortune’s Wheel** (when the ritual window is open)
- **Fortune Ledger** (collapsible, paged history of spins)
- Guild Heroes / spotlight when offered

## Magical Analytics

Not for grading. For **coaching**: which classes feed which house, who the quiet contributors are, whether one hero is carrying a crystal. Celebrate contribution percentage as teamwork, not as a league table of children.

## Grand Guild Ceremony

End-of-year spectacle (Home shows the call when it is time). It looks back across the year:

- Hero of the Day legends
- Team Quest journeys
- Prodigy timeline
- Fortune’s Wheel
- Familiars
- Guild Champions

Then it crowns the winning guild by **Guild Power**. Music, drumrolls, the same dignity rules as the monthly ceremony: the climb matters.

This is **not** Ceremony of the Month. Do not run the monthly dual ritual and call it the Guild Ceremony.

## Fortune’s Wheel

A weekly ritual, not a slot machine to mash every lesson. It lives on this tab because the spin writes **Glory** — but it comes **after** the houses, the quiz, and the year-long race.

**When it may spin**

- A class is selected
- It is that class’s **last lesson day of the Monday–Friday week**
- You are **inside lesson time**
- This class has **not** already spun this week

The wheel draws **20** unique segments from a large catalogue. Categories:

- **Glory** — instant Glory, multipliers, shields, unity bonuses
- **Perk** — stars, Gold, artifacts, Team Quest bonuses for the class
- **Fun** — anthem, confetti, small Glory
- **Negative** — Glory tax, slumber, heist, lost Gold/stars/artifacts, Trickster (nothing happens)

Junior leagues **soften cursed** outcomes so young children are not crushed by Calamity. **Bulwark Crest** and Wheel **Glory Shield** can block negative Glory hits. **Shattered Mirror** halves the *next positive* Wheel effect.

Spin **with the class watching**. Read the Ledger afterward so the story is remembered.

## Fortune Ledger

History of what the Wheel actually did: who gained Glory, who found an artifact, which class got a Quest bonus. Use it in the Grand Guild Ceremony narrative and in parent conversations (“the house had a hard Wheel week, then recovered”).

## How this feeds the rest of the Quest

| Input | Guild effect |
|-------|----------------|
| Award Stars | Glory at 2:1, Power updates |
| Hero’s Boon (+0.5) | Glory for the receiver’s house |
| Quiz of the Week | Extra Glory; legendary tier can bless the leading house for 24h |
| Glory artifacts / Wheel | Spikes, shields, drama |
| Pathfinder / Quest bonuses | Team Quest, not Power directly — but Wheel perks can add both |

Hero’s Challenge still sorts **students**, not guilds. Send the class to Guild Hall to *feel* the house race.
