# Quest Calendar

Quest Calendar is the **month grid of the teaching year**: lesson days, star totals, holidays, cancelled days, and **Quest Events**. Tap a day to open the **Day Planner**.

## Purpose

Keep the Quest honest about time. A month with Easter week should not demand the same Team Quest goal as a full teaching month. Special lesson types should be visible *before* the children sit down.

## What you see (desktop)

A month calendar. Cells show:

- Whether this is a **lesson day** (from class schedules plus one-time overrides)
- **Star totals** logged that day
- Icons for **Quest Events**
- Themes for **school holidays** and **no-school** days (for example Winter Break)

On **mobile**, the same tab is a **day agenda** with Plan / Logbook, not a cramped month grid.

## Day Planner

Two tabs: **Schedule** and **Quest Event**.

### Schedule

- List of classes that meet this day
- **Cancel** a lesson (your class) — this is a schedule override; it lowers Team Quest goal pressure
- **Add a one-time lesson** — pick a class chip, then **Add lesson**
- **Mark as School Holiday** for a single unexpected closure

School-wide **holiday ranges** (Christmas, Easter, generic) are created in the **School Office** (Secretary). Teachers do not own the whole-school calendar in Settings anymore. **My Planning** is only each class’s **final lesson day**.

The Day Planner still has **Mark School Holiday** for a **single date**: it cancels **all classes** that day and reshapes goals. Use it for an unexpected one-day closure; use the School Office for Winter Break and Easter.

### Quest Event

Choose a card for that date, pick the classes, then **Summon Event**. Two families:

**Standard events (the app applies them to Award Stars automatically)**

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **2× Star Day** | Every positive star award that day is **doubled** |
| **Reason Bonus Day** | Pick Teamwork, Creativity, Respect, or Focus. Matching awards get **+1 extra star** |

**Special quests (you run them in the room; the calendar stores the goal and bonus)**

| Quest | You set | In the lesson |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Vocabulary Vault** | Goal (uses/sentences) + completion bonus stars | Students spend the Word / target words in real English. Count toward the vault. |
| **The Unbroken Chain** | Completion bonus | Fluency: keep a spoken chain going without collapse. |
| **Grammar Guardians** | Goal + completion bonus | Find and mend errors; “rescue” sentences. |
| **The Scribe’s Sketch** | Completion bonus | Listen and draw what you hear. |
| **Five-Sentence Saga** | Completion bonus | A tiny constrained story (five sentences, given ingredients). |

Delete an event from the calendar when plans change.

Each Special Quest belongs to one class and can be continued later. If several
classes are selected, the app keeps a separate event for each class. When the
quest starts, the app checks attendance. When it is completed, each chosen child
receives the selected Stars and the same amount of Gold once — even if the button
is pressed twice or the connection briefly drops. Undo keeps a clear record of
what was taken back and is available only while the reward Gold is still there
to recover. Special rewards contribute to Team Quest, Guild Glory and Familiars,
but never to ordinary daily locking, Hero Path, Bounty, 2×/Reason modifiers,
artifacts or Hero of the Day.

### The five Quest shapes (in the lesson)

The teacher uses the lesson screen to move the quest forward. The runner’s **Projector** button previews the class-facing quest display (badge, optional prompt, progress bar — no teacher buttons). **Controls** returns to the runner. That preview is not the header **TV** **Projector Mode** wallpaper.

The teacher runner has a themed banner
(quest badge, Active or Completed, and Stars / Gold per recipient), a Quest
Objective bar, a live widget (vault gems, grammar runes, chain links, scribe
checklist, or saga pages), then Reward Recipients. The app remembers where you
stopped, so you can continue after a refresh. Default goals are deliberately
small and can be changed only within the stated range:

| Quest | Goal and controls | Completion |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| **Vocabulary Vault** | 10 valid uses (range 5–30). **Add Word Gem** counts a use. **Complete Quest** stays off until the goal. Vault gems show on the teacher screen. | When the goal is reached |
| **Grammar Guardians** | 8 rescued sentences (range 3–20). **Rescued Sentence** counts a rescue. | When the goal is reached |
| **The Unbroken Chain** | 10 successful turns (range 5–30). **Successful Turn**, **Chain Broke** (the current chain starts again; the best chain remains). | When the goal is reached |
| **The Scribe’s Sketch** | Four buttons: **Step 1: Listen**, **Step 2: Sketch**, **Step 3: Add details**, **Step 4: Reveal & describe**. The optional listening prompt is at most 160 characters and can be shown on the projector only by opt-in. | All four stages complete |
| **Five-Sentence Saga** | Exactly five sentence slots. **Submit Sentence** locks the previous slot; each sentence is optional and at most 240 characters. | Fifth sentence completed |

The lesson screen remembers progress after a refresh. At Start, the teacher confirms
who is present: recorded absences are left out; without attendance, all active
learners are selected to begin with. Children can still be added or removed
before Complete. The selected reward is 0.5, 1, 1.5, or 2 Stars per recipient,
with the same amount of Gold. After Complete, **Undo** reverses the whole
completion only when every recipient still has that Gold available. There is no
partial Stars-only undo.

Scheduling rules keep a day unambiguous: one standard modifier per class/day,
one active Special Quest per class, no duplicate quest of the same kind on the
same day, and no repeating events in this release. A school-wide selection makes
one event for each class. Holiday, cancelled, or no-lesson conflicts are shown
before save; overdue events become **Missed** and may be moved only to the next
real lesson date. Older Special Quests without a class are shown as **Needs class
assignment** and cannot start.

These special quests are **not** the same as Story Weavers (a whole tab) or Quiz of the Week (Home). They are **one-day lesson shapes**.

## How this feeds the rest of the Quest

- Holidays and cancellations **reshape monthly Team Quest goals**.
- 2× / Reason Bonus days **reshape Gold and ranks** that day — announce them so the class understands the “weather.”
- Quest Board tests scheduled via assignments show as academic alerts, not as these event types.
- Home and Projector can countdown holidays and tests.
