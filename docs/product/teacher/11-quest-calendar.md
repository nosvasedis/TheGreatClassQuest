# Quest Calendar

Quest Calendar is the **month grid of the teaching year**: lesson days, star totals, holidays, cancelled days, and **Quest Events**. Tap a day to open the **Day Planner**.

## Purpose

Keep the Quest honest about time. A month with Easter week should not demand the same Team Quest goal as a full teaching month. Special lesson types should be visible *before* the children sit down.

## What you see (desktop)

A month calendar. Cells show:

- Whether this is a **lesson day** (from class schedules plus one-time overrides)
- **Star totals** logged that day
- Icons for **Quest Events**
- Themes for **school holidays** and **no-school** days (for example Winter Break)

On **mobile**, the same tab is a **day agenda** with Plan / Logbook, not a cramped month grid.

## Day Planner

Two tabs: **Schedule** and **Quest Event**.

### Schedule

- List of classes that meet this day
- **Cancel** a lesson of your class. The class meets less that month, so its Team Quest goal is lowered
- **Add a one-time lesson** — pick a class chip, then **Add lesson**
- **Mark as School Holiday** for a single unexpected closure

School-wide **holiday ranges** (Christmas, Easter, generic) are created in the **School Office** (Secretary). Teachers do not edit the whole-school calendar in Settings; that is the School Office’s job. **My Planning** is only each class’s **final lesson day**.

The Day Planner still has **Mark School Holiday** for a **single date**: it cancels **all classes** that day and reshapes goals. Use it for an unexpected one-day closure; use the School Office for Winter Break and Easter.

### Quest Event

Choose a card for that date, pick the classes (standard events cover every class), then **Summon Event**. Two families:

**Standard events (the app applies them to Award Stars automatically)**

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **2× Star Day** | Every positive star award that day is **doubled** |
| **Reason Bonus Day** | Pick Teamwork, Creativity, Respect, or Focus. Matching awards get **+1 extra star** |

**Special quests (you run them in the room; the calendar stores the goal and bonus)**

| Quest | You set | In the lesson |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Vocabulary Vault** | Goal (uses/sentences) + completion bonus stars | Students use the target words in real English; each use counts toward the vault. |
| **The Unbroken Chain** | Completion bonus | Fluency: keep a spoken chain going without collapse. |
| **Grammar Guardians** | Goal + completion bonus | Find and mend errors; “rescue” sentences. |
| **The Scribe’s Sketch** | Completion bonus | Listen and draw what you hear. |
| **Five-Sentence Saga** | Completion bonus | A tiny constrained story (five sentences, given ingredients). |

Delete an event from the calendar when plans change.

Each Special Quest belongs to one class and can be continued later. If you choose several classes, each one gets its own event. When a quest starts, the app checks who is present. When you complete it, each chosen child receives the stars you selected and the same amount of Gold, once only, even if you press the button twice. **Undo** is possible only while the Gold is still there to take back, and it keeps a clear record. Special Quest rewards count toward Team Quest, Guild Glory and Familiars, but they are not ordinary daily awards: they never affect Hero Path, Bounties, 2× / Reason Bonus Day, artifacts or Hero of the Day.

### The five Quest shapes (in the lesson)

The teacher uses the lesson screen to move the quest forward. The runner’s **Projector** button previews the class-facing quest display (badge, optional prompt, progress bar — no teacher buttons). **Controls** returns to the runner. That preview is not the header **TV** **Projector Mode** wallpaper.

The quest screen has a themed banner (quest badge, Active or Completed, and Stars / Gold per child), a Quest Objective bar, a live display (vault gems, grammar runes, chain links, scribe checklist or saga pages), and then the list of children who will receive the reward. The app remembers where you stopped, so you can carry on after a refresh. The default goals are deliberately small; you can change them within the ranges below:

| Quest | Goal and controls | Completion |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| **Vocabulary Vault** | 10 valid uses (range 5–30). **Add Word Gem** counts a use. **Complete Quest** stays off until the goal. Vault gems show on the teacher screen. | When the goal is reached |
| **Grammar Guardians** | 8 rescued sentences (range 3–20). **Rescued Sentence** counts a rescue. | When the goal is reached |
| **The Unbroken Chain** | 10 successful turns (range 5–30). **Successful Turn**, **Chain Broke** (the current chain starts again; the best chain remains). | When the goal is reached |
| **The Scribe’s Sketch** | Four buttons: **Step 1: Listen**, **Step 2: Sketch**, **Step 3: Add details**, **Step 4: Reveal & describe**. You can add a short listening prompt (up to 160 characters); it appears on the projector only if you choose to show it. | All four stages complete |
| **Five-Sentence Saga** | Exactly five sentence slots. **Submit Sentence** closes the previous sentence; each sentence is optional and can be up to 240 characters. | Fifth sentence completed |

When you start, you confirm who is present: children with a recorded absence are left out, and if no attendance has been taken, all active students start selected. You can add or remove children before you press Complete. The reward is 0.5, 1, 1.5 or 2 stars per child, with the same amount of Gold. After Complete, **Undo** reverses the whole completion, and only when every child still has that Gold available; there is no stars-only undo.

A few rules keep the calendar clear: only one standard event per day; no second quest of the same kind for the same class on the same day; and events do not repeat. Standard events (**2× Star Day** and **Reason Bonus Day**) are **school-wide**: the Day Planner shows a single **All classes** chip, and the event applies to every class that meets that day. Special Quests stay per class, so choosing several classes makes one event for each. If a chosen class has no lesson that day (holiday, cancelled, or not its day), the app names it and does not save until you sort out the conflict. Older Special Quests that were saved without a class cannot be started.

These special quests are **not** the same as Story Weavers (a whole tab) or Quiz of the Week (Home). They are **one-day lesson shapes**.

## How this feeds the rest of the Quest

- Holidays and cancellations **reshape monthly Team Quest goals**.
- 2× / Reason Bonus days **change Gold and ranks** that day — announce them so every class expects the bonus.
- Quest Board tests scheduled via assignments show as academic alerts, not as these event types.
- Home and Projector can countdown holidays and tests.
