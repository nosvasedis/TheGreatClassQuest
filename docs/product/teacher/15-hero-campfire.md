# Hero Campfire and Ember Oaths

The **Hero Campfire** is an optional, two-minute closing ritual on the projector. The class gathers around an animated campfire, remembers the words it practised, answers one reflection question, shows how brightly it learned, and checks in on a few **Ember Oaths**: small personal promises each child chose. There are no ranks, no scores and no Gold here.

## Purpose

Stars recognise actions. The Campfire adds the two habits the rest of the Quest does not cover: **looking back at what was learned** and **working towards a personal goal**. Both are among the most effective, lowest-cost habits in education research (metacognition and self-regulation; short, specific, self-chosen goals). The child chooses the goal, the teacher confirms progress, and the reward is a shared light, not a number.

## When it appears

- The Campfire is prepared **quietly**: writing the Quest Assignment (for next time) starts the pack in the background, and the last 15 minutes of a lesson (or after half the lesson once stars have been awarded) finishes today’s script. It needs no waiting. On **Elite**, AI may polish the reflection question and closing lines, and may reorder or trim the word list — it never invents a word.
- When you close the Hero of the Day celebration (**Huzzah!**), **Gather at the Campfire** lights up in the Adventure Log, under Log Today’s Adventure and Hall of Heroes, where the Ember Oaths button usually sits. A small pill also appears on Home. The hearth sits in the same bubbly family as **🔥 Ember Oaths**; the Oaths chip on it is that same rose-ember button, with a **ready** badge when promises can be kept.
- It never opens by itself. The small **×** on it means “not today”. It is on for every class; there is nothing to switch on.
- After the ritual the button rests as **Campfire held · Relight**.

## What the class sees (seven moments)

You move on with **Space, Enter or →**, go back with **←**, and close with **Esc**. Moments with nothing to show are skipped. Nothing is spoken aloud; everything is visual.

| Moment | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Kindling** | The fire is only sleeping coals. Press **✦ Light our fire**: today's stars fall one by one into the coals. A gold plaque sits above the hearth and brightens as they land — then it blooms: **✦ N stars lit our fire tonight**. After that it shrinks and rests as a tiny caption under the **Hero Campfire** title, and it stays there for the rest of the ritual. |
| **Word Embers** | The words the children practised glow like embers, each with a tiny task (say it, act it, use it in a sentence — or, only when the word has a known classroom opposite, find the opposite). When the book gives an example sentence, it sits under the word. On Elite, a picture of a *concrete* thing (a lion, a sandwich — never an idea like *discover*) may appear, prepared when you wrote the assignment. Tap one when the class has used it: it flies into the fire. Grammar-book lessons with no word list show **tonight’s pattern** instead of a fake topic. |
| **The Question** | One reflection question about the lesson, with two "Try starting with…" sentence starters. **Primary Path** units share one Big Question: the first lesson of a unit asks how they would answer it today; later lessons in that unit may ask whether the answer has changed. **Yeti** units are one lesson each and stay on those words. **Grammar books** (Burlington My Grammar Book, Grammalysis) ask about the pattern (e.g. *have got*), never a made-up theme. Talk about it; there is nothing to press. |
| **Class Glow** | The class shows 🔥 I tried, 🕯️ Still growing or 🌙 A quiet day with their hands; tap the majority. Each choice has its own colour, and the fire answers. |
| **Oath Circle** | Three or four children sit around the fire with their avatars and a speech bubble with their promise (or “a secret promise”). Tap 🔥 / 🕯️ / 🌙 under each child: that is **today’s check-in**, the same as on the Oath Board — not the keep. The avatar glows in that colour with a burst of sparks (🔥), a warm glow (🕯️) or twinkling stars (🌙). The Hero of the Day wears a crown. |
| **Promises Kept** | A promise is ready only when there is enough **evidence** (Award Stars for a virtue, logged practice, quiz, or a moment you tapped) **and** at least one 🔥 check-in. Then tap **✨ Let it rise**: an ember climbs from the fire and blooms into a golden Star-Ember with the child’s name. **Place it in our sky** sends it to that kind of promise’s own figure in the class sky. The six kinds match the Oath Board exactly: speaking, new words, writing, reading & listening, a good habit, a hero virtue. Each kind has its own patch of sky and its own way of joining (a path, a tree, a hub, or a loop) — never a mixed-kind line. A secret promise stays a quiet gold spark, so the class does not read the type. A small key names the kinds that are shining. |
| **Embers Sleep** | A closing line and a hint about next time, in plain words (the next unit's topic or big question), never book codes or pages. |

The fire has a warm crackling sound (🔊 at the top mutes it; your choice is remembered). No book names, units, pages or timers appear on the projector.

**Look up.** A small telescope sits with the mute and full-screen buttons. Tap it at any moment: the camera lifts to the same stars already in the sky and zooms that map — never a new arrangement. Drag to wander, **+** / **−** or the mouse wheel to draw closer or further, hover a star to read who kept what (or “A promise kept” for a secret). **Look down**, **Esc**, or **Continue** returns to the fire. Nursery and Pre-Junior see no names on the stars.

**Nursery and Pre-Junior (Growth Festival rules):** no names and no individual oaths on the projector. The circle becomes **Our Class Promise** with a “warm our hands together” moment; the Class Glow uses faces.

## How it knows what the class did

You write each Quest Assignment at the END of a lesson, for the next one. So at the end of the next lesson, the Campfire shows the assignment written at the end of the PREVIOUS lesson: what the children practised at home. The new assignment you write today is only used for the gentle “next time” line. It works whether you write the new assignment before or after the Campfire, and if you re-save today’s assignment it simply replaces it.

- **Words** (4–6), in this order: words you wrote after `words:`, `φωτοτυπία:`, `worksheet:` or `elab:`; the unit’s wordlist (preferring the pages you set); then today’s Quiz, Story Weavers and Special Quest words. On a **senior course** (e.g. Close-Up), each unit teaches a *different* vocabulary set on each page set, so the fire uses exactly the pages you assigned, not the whole unit. When the class used **more than one book** that day (a coursebook and a grammar book, or a Student’s Book and an activity book / companion), the fire draws words from **every** book and part it was given. A junior course (Primary Path, Yeti, Bamboo) is taught as a whole lesson, so the unit’s words are used together. Instructions, page numbers and repeats are filtered out. On Elite, AI may only reorder or trim this list; it never adds a word. Word-ember tasks are local: “Opposite?” appears only for a small classroom antonym list (hot/cold, yesterday/tomorrow…), never for words like *discover*.
- **The question** is anchored in the lesson: a test or quiz played today, then the unit’s Big Question (Primary Path — the same question for the whole unit, with “has your answer changed?” only after the class has lived with it), its topic (Close-Up), its words (Yeti’s smaller units), or its grammar point (Burlington My Grammar Book, Grammalysis — never treated as a discussion theme). Questions used in the last 10 Campfires of that class are skipped.
- Books in the atlas: Cambridge Primary Path 1–3, New Close-Up B1 and B1+, Yeti 2, English with Bamboo, Burlington My Grammar Book 1–3, Grammalysis B1 and B2. For each, a page can be recognised in the part the class used — the Student’s Book, the Activity book, the Companion / Language booster, or the Grammar book — so `PP2 activity book p.15` and `yeti 2 language booster p.108` land on the right unit. Any other book works through **Αλλαγή → Other / custom book** with your own theme.
- If the book is unclear and you did not confirm it, the Campfire still uses the homework words, but it never changes the class’s book.

## Ember Oaths

Open the **Oath Board** from **🔥 Ember Oaths** in the Adventure Log (it follows the class in the header, and stays greyed out until a class is selected), the 📜 Oaths part of the lit Campfire button, **🔥 Oaths** on a class card in My Classes (just left of the trash can), or the **Oaths** page tab in a student’s Hero’s Chronicle.

- **The board.** One card per child with their photo/avatar, their promise, glowing embers for progress and today’s check-in (🔥 Tried it · 🕯️ Growing · 🌙 Quiet day). Filters: Everyone · Need a promise · Ready to keep.
- **Choosing ceremony (one tap per child).** Press **✨ Choosing ceremony**. One child at a time appears (a row of small avatars at the top shows who already has a promise 🔥). Three coloured promise cards are chosen for THAT child from what the app knows: which virtue they earn least (or most, to share it), their Hero Class, their dictation and test trend, their Quiz of the Week accuracy, recent absences, how their stars compare with the class, the real words and topic of the current unit, and their earlier promises (never repeated). Each card says why in one line. **🔄 Other ideas** shows three more of different kinds, so all six Oath Board kinds can appear. Read them aloud, the child picks, you tap **🔥 Light this promise**, and the next child appears. **Their own words** lets you type exactly what the child says. **🔒 Secret promise** shows “a secret promise” at the Campfire instead of the words. The length is set for you (1 week for Nursery–Junior, 2 weeks after).
- **Moments (evidence).** Many fill themselves: a virtue promise grows every time you award that virtue in Award Stars; practice promises grow with logged dictations/tests. Open a card to add a moment with one tap (e.g. “Spoke up in English”) or write your own.
- **Keeping.** The three campfire orbs (and the same 🔥 Tried it · 🕯️ Growing · 🌙 Quiet day on the board) are **today’s check-in**. They do not, by themselves, mark a promise kept. When every ember of **evidence** glows (Award Stars for a virtue promise, logged dictations/tests, quiz, or a moment you added) **and** there has been at least one 🔥 check-in, the card says **Ready to keep ✨**. Open it, tap how the child feels (🌱 ✨ 💪 😊), optionally note what helped, and **Keep the promise**. At the Campfire the same ready promise shows **✨ Let it rise**. The child receives a **Star-Ember** keepsake in the Trophy Room and a line in Hero’s Chronicle (Goals). No stars, no Gold. Then you can **Choose the next promise** straight away.
- **Letting go.** “Let this promise go” ends an oath with no penalty.
- **Family Portal.** For a kept oath, **Review for Family Portal** lets you publish one short message you have read. Evidence, check-ins and reflections never leave the teacher.

## How it feeds the rest of the Quest

- Hero’s Chronicle (Goals notes) and the Oracle (Elite) see oaths and reflections; parent summaries leave private oaths out.
- The class constellation grows all year: one star per kept oath, each kind in its own figure. The telescope lets the class hover above them.
- The Hero of the Day sits on the crowned stone; guild colours spark when a child checks in with 🔥.
