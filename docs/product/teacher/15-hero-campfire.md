# Hero Campfire and Ember Oaths

The **Hero Campfire** is an optional, two-minute closing ritual on the projector. The class gathers around an animated campfire, remembers the words it practised, answers one reflection question, shows how brightly it learned, and checks in on a few **Ember Oaths**: small personal promises each child chose. There are no ranks, no scores and no Gold here.

## Purpose

Stars recognise actions. The Campfire adds the two habits the rest of the Quest does not cover: **looking back at what was learned** and **working towards a personal goal**. Education research ranks both among the most effective low-cost habits: thinking about your own learning, and working towards short, specific goals you chose yourself. The child chooses the goal, the teacher confirms progress, and the reward is a shared light, not a number.

## When it appears

- The Campfire prepares itself in the background. When you save a Quest Assignment it starts getting the next lesson’s campfire ready, and in the last 15 minutes of a lesson (or after half the lesson, once stars have been awarded) it finishes today’s. You never have to wait for it. On **Elite**, AI may polish the reflection question and closing lines, and may reorder or trim the word list, but it never invents a word.
- When you close the Hero of the Day celebration (**Huzzah!**) and choose how to write today’s page (Auto, Manual or Later), **Gather at the Campfire** lights up in the Adventure Log, under Crown Today’s Hero and Hall of Heroes, where the Ember Oaths button usually sits. A small **Hero Campfire** badge also appears first among the reminder badges in the Home greeting card; tap it to gather there. The Campfire button sits where the **🔥 Ember Oaths** button usually is, and carries a small **Oaths** chip (with a **ready** badge when promises can be kept) that opens the Oath Board.
- It never opens by itself. The small **×** on it (in the Adventure Log and on the Home badge) means “not today” and hides both until tomorrow. It is on for every class; there is nothing to switch on.
- After the ritual the button rests as **Campfire held · Relight**.

## What the class sees (seven moments)

You move on with **Space, Enter or →**, go back with **←**, and close with **Esc**. Moments with nothing to show are skipped. Nothing is spoken aloud; everything is visual.

| Moment | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Kindling** | The fire is only sleeping coals. Press **✦ Light our fire**: today's stars fall one by one into the coals. A gold plaque sits above the hearth and brightens as they land — then it blooms: **✦ N stars lit our fire tonight**. After that it shrinks and rests as a tiny caption under the **Hero Campfire** title, and it stays there for the rest of the ritual. |
| **Word Embers** | The words the children practised glow like embers, each with a tiny task (say it, act it, use it in a sentence — or, only when the word has a known classroom opposite, find the opposite). When the book gives an example sentence, it sits under the word. On Elite, a picture of a *concrete* thing (a lion, a sandwich — never an idea like *discover*) may appear, prepared when you wrote the assignment. Tap one when the class has used it: it flies into the fire. Grammar-book lessons with no word list show **tonight’s pattern** instead of a fake topic. |
| **The Question** | One reflection question about the lesson, with two "Try starting with…" sentence starters. **Primary Path** units share one Big Question: the first lesson of a unit asks how they would answer it today; later lessons in that unit may ask whether the answer has changed. **Yeti** units are one lesson each and stay on those words. **Grammar books** (Burlington My Grammar Book, Grammalysis) ask about the pattern (e.g. *have got*), never a made-up theme. Talk about it; there is nothing to press. |
| **Class Glow** | The class shows 🔥 I tried, 🕯️ Still growing or 🌙 A quiet day with their hands; tap the majority. Each choice has its own colour, and the fire answers. |
| **Oath Circle** | Three or four children sit around the fire with their avatars and a speech bubble with their promise (or “a secret promise”). Tap 🔥 / 🕯️ / 🌙 under each child: that is **today’s check-in**, the same as on the Oath Board — not the keep. The avatar glows in that colour with a burst of sparks (🔥), a warm glow (🕯️) or twinkling stars (🌙). The Hero of the Day wears a crown. |
| **Promises Kept** | A promise is ready only when there is enough **evidence** (Award Stars for a virtue, logged practice, quiz, or a moment you tapped) **and** at least one 🔥 check-in. Then tap **✨ Let it rise**: an ember climbs from the fire and blooms into a golden Star-Ember with the child’s name. **Place it in our sky** adds it to the class sky, in the figure for that kind of promise. The six kinds match the Oath Board exactly: speaking, new words, writing, reading & listening, a good habit, a hero virtue. Each kind has its own patch of sky and its own way of joining the stars (a path, a tree, a hub or a loop), so kinds never mix in one line. A secret promise stays a quiet gold spark, so the class cannot tell what kind it was. A small key names the kinds that are shining. |
| **Embers Sleep** | A closing line and a hint about next time, in plain words (the next unit's topic or big question), never book codes or pages. |

The fire has a warm crackling sound (🔊 at the top mutes it; your choice is remembered). No book names, units, pages or timers appear on the projector.

**Look up.** A small telescope sits with the mute and full-screen buttons. Tap it at any moment: the camera lifts to the same stars already in the sky and zooms that map — never a new arrangement. Drag to wander, **+** / **−** or the mouse wheel to draw closer or further, hover a star to read who kept what (or “A promise kept” for a secret). **Look down**, **Esc**, or **Continue** returns to the fire. Pre-Junior sees no names on the stars.

**Pre-Junior (Growth Festival rules):** no names and no individual oaths on the projector. The circle becomes **Our Class Promise** with a “warm our hands together” moment; the Class Glow uses faces.

## How it knows what the class did

You write each Quest Assignment at the **end** of a lesson, for the next one. So at the end of the next lesson, the Campfire looks back at the assignment from the **previous** lesson: what the children practised at home. The assignment you write today is used only for the gentle “next time” line. It does not matter whether you write the new assignment before or after the Campfire; re-saving today’s assignment simply replaces it.

- **Words** (4–6), chosen in this order: words you wrote after `words:`, `φωτοτυπία:`, `worksheet:` or `elab:`; then the unit’s word list (preferring the pages you set); then today’s Quiz, Story Weavers and Special Quest words. Instructions, page numbers and repeats are filtered out. On a **senior course** (for example Close-Up), each unit teaches a different set of words on each page set, so the Campfire uses exactly the pages you assigned, not the whole unit. When the class used **more than one book** that day (a coursebook and a grammar book, or a Student’s Book and an activity book or companion), words come from **every** book and part it was given. A junior course (Primary Path, Yeti, Bamboo) is taught as a whole lesson, so the unit’s words are used together. On Elite, AI may only reorder or trim the list; it never adds a word. The word tasks are fixed, not made up by AI: “Opposite?” appears only for a short classroom list of true opposites (hot/cold, yesterday/tomorrow…), never for words like *discover*.
- **The question** is tied to the lesson, taken from the first of these that applies: a test or quiz played today; the unit’s Big Question (Primary Path: the same question all unit, with “has your answer changed?” only once the class has lived with it); its topic (Close-Up); its words (Yeti’s smaller units); or its grammar point (Burlington My Grammar Book, Grammalysis, never treated as a discussion theme). Questions used in that class’s last 10 Campfires are skipped.
- Books the app recognises: Cambridge Primary Path 1–3, New Close-Up B1, Yeti 2, English with Bamboo, Burlington My Grammar Book 1–3, Grammalysis B1. For each, a page can be recognised in the part the class used — the Student’s Book, the Activity book, the Companion / Language booster, or the Grammar book — so `PP2 activity book p.15` and `yeti 2 language booster p.108` land on the right unit. Any other book works through **Another book…**, where you give a short theme of your own.
- If the book is unclear and you did not confirm it, the Campfire still uses the homework words, but it never changes the class’s book.

## Ember Oaths

Open the **Oath Board** from **🔥 Ember Oaths** in the Adventure Log (it follows the class in the header, and stays greyed out until a class is selected), the 📜 Oaths part of the lit Campfire button, **🔥 Oaths** on a class card in My Classes (just left of the trash can), or the **Oaths** page tab in a student’s Hero’s Chronicle.

- **The board.** One card per child with their photo/avatar, their promise, glowing embers for progress and today’s check-in (🔥 Tried it · 🕯️ Growing · 🌙 Quiet day). Filters: Everyone · Need a promise · Ready to keep.
- **Choosing ceremony (one tap per child).** Press **✨ Choosing ceremony**. One child at a time appears (a row of small avatars at the top shows who already has a promise 🔥). Three coloured promise cards are suggested for that child from what the app knows: the virtue they earn least (or most, to share it), their Hero Class, their dictation and test trend, their Quiz of the Week accuracy, recent absences, how their stars compare with the class, the real words and topic of the current unit, and their earlier promises (never repeated). Each card says why in one line. **🔄 Other ideas** shows three more of different kinds, so all six Oath Board kinds can appear. Read them aloud, the child picks, you tap **🔥 Light this promise**, and the next child appears. **Their own words** lets you type exactly what the child says. **🔒 Secret promise** shows “a secret promise” at the Campfire instead of the words. The length is set for you (1 week for Pre-Junior to Junior B, 2 weeks after).
- **Moments (evidence).** Many fill themselves: a virtue promise grows every time you award that virtue in Award Stars; practice promises grow with logged dictations/tests. Open a card to add a moment with one tap (e.g. “Spoke up in English”) or write your own.
- **Keeping.** The three campfire orbs (and the same 🔥 Tried it · 🕯️ Growing · 🌙 Quiet day on the board) are **today’s check-in**. They do not, by themselves, mark a promise kept. When every ember of **evidence** glows (Award Stars for a virtue promise, logged dictations/tests, quiz, or a moment you added) **and** there has been at least one 🔥 check-in, the card says **Ready to keep ✨**. Open it, tap how the child feels (🌱 ✨ 💪 😊), optionally note what helped, and **Keep the promise**. At the Campfire the same ready promise shows **✨ Let it rise**. The child receives a **Star-Ember** keepsake in the Trophy Room and a line in Hero’s Chronicle (Goals). No stars, no Gold. Then you can **Choose the next promise** straight away.
- **Letting go.** “Let this promise go” ends an oath with no penalty.
- **Family Portal.** For a kept oath, **Review for Family Portal** lets you publish one short message you have read. Evidence, check-ins and reflections never leave the teacher.

## How it feeds the rest of the Quest

- Hero’s Chronicle (Goals notes) and the Oracle (Elite) see oaths and reflections; parent summaries leave private oaths out.
- The class constellation grows all year: one star per kept oath, each kind in its own figure. The telescope lets the class hover above them.
- The Hero of the Day sits on the crowned stone; guild colours spark when a child checks in with 🔥.
