# Quest Remote (the Wand)

**Quest Remote** turns your phone into a **Wand** for the classroom projector. You walk around the room, and every tap or gesture on your phone appears on the big screen. A spark flies in from the bottom edge to the thing you touched, then the app does exactly what the mouse would have done. Pro and Elite.

## Purpose

You should not have to go back to the keyboard to praise a child. With the Wand you can award a star while standing next to the pair who earned it, crown today's hero from the back of the room, or run a team showdown while you move between the desks. The class sees the magic arrive on the screen. You stay the judge: nothing happens unless you do it.

## Waking the Wand

1. On the classroom computer, press the **wand** button in the header (next to Projector Mode). In Projector Mode, use the wand button in the remote or the **W** key.
2. A rune circle with a QR code opens. Point your phone's camera at it.
3. Your phone opens the app, signed in as you, and binds. The runes light up, a beam rises on the projector and your phone vibrates.

**No camera?** On your phone, open **More › Quest Remote**. It lists your projectors that are awake. Match the four runes shown under the circle.

The header wand is the Wand's status light. A turning lilac ring means it is waiting for your phone. A gold wand with a gentle glow means your phone is bound. Click it at any time to see the circle again, or to **put the Wand to sleep**. If the classroom computer reloads the page, the session comes back by itself.

Only your own phone, signed in as you, can command your projector. Nothing is shown to anyone else.

## The five modes on your phone

| Mode | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Stars** | The class as hero orbs (today's stars, who is away). Tap a hero, choose the virtue (Teamwork, Creativity, Respect, Focus), then **flick the star up** towards the screen. With **Flick** chosen, a faster flick gives more stars (1–3). You can also fix the size (★, ★★ or ★★★) and just tap. **Several** lets you tap a few heroes who earned it together (a pair, a table) and give them all the same star in one flick. **Undo** stays on your phone for a few seconds. A shine ring shows how many heroes have a star today, and **Still to shine** lists only the present heroes who have none yet, so nobody is forgotten (the Wand remembers that choice). In a hero's card you can also mark them away, present, or give the Welcome Back bonus, or **Spotlight** them: the room dims and a beam falls on the hero (a star for a spotlit hero lifts the lights first). Stars work from any screen: if the projector shows something else, Award Stars opens and a comet flies into the hero's cloud. If a window covers the screen (Projector Mode, a show), a golden ribbon with the hero's name drops in instead, and the star is saved on their cloud underneath. |
| **Screen** | The same clouds as the bottom of the projector: tap one to show that screen (Projector Mode steps aside first). On the projector the matching cloud rises, even when the dock is resting out of sight, and the Wand touches it. Below are **the controls of whatever is on screen** (a window, a ceremony, the Market, the Training Grounds), in the order you read them on the projector and under the same headings. They look like the real ones: same colours and shapes, tabs as a row of tabs with the open one lit, on/off switches, dropdowns with the same choices, and text boxes you type into with your phone's keyboard (**Send** puts the text into the projector's box). A tiny screen on each button shows where it sits; an arrow means it is scrolled out of sight (the projector scrolls to it). **Back** closes the top window, the arrows scroll (they rest at the top and bottom, and a bar shows how far down you are), **OK** is Enter. When Fortune's Wheel, the Fair Picker, Projector Mode or the quiz is open, their controls come first. |
| **Magic** | **Crown Today's Hero**: hold the crown until the ring fills. **Fortune's Wheel**: open it, then **pull the lever down and let go**. A harder pull gives a wilder spin; the result is still the wheel's own luck. **Fair Picker**: shake your phone (or tap) to summon the next hero. **Quiet Dragon** and **Projector Mode** start from here too. **Sound charms**: Ta-daa, drum roll, fanfare, ding ding, buzzer and wah-wah play on the projector's speakers, with a comic word for the class to see. If the projector's sound is still locked (browsers wait for a first click on the computer), the Wand tells you. |
| **Lesson** | The running of the room. **Timers**: Think 30 s · Pair 60 s · Share 90 s, 2 or 5 minutes, or **your own timer** from 1 to 30 minutes, shown as a large ember ring on the projector (pause, +30 s, stop; the last five seconds tick aloud). **Eyes on me**: velvet curtains close over the projector; lift them from the phone, or click the projector. **Look here**: a small map of the projector on your phone. Tap a spot and a beacon pulses there, so the class knows where to look. |
| **Show** | **Showdown Arena** (below), and the **Quiz host console** when Quiz of the Week is on screen (Elite). |

A strip under the top of the Wand always shows what the class sees (and a running timer). Tap it to jump to **Screen**. The Wand remembers the mode you used last. Tap the wand crest at the top to see your **recent spells**: what the projector did with your last taps.

**Which class?** The class button on the Wand shows the projector's class, never your phone's own. It has the same **Follow today's schedule** switch and **General view** (no class, the whole school) as the header's class compass, and the two stay in step. **Crown Today's Hero** follows the diary's own rules: with no class or no stars yet it shows what is missing, and once the hero is crowned it becomes **Write today's page** / **Open today's page**.

Settings, the student roster and other forms are never cast to the projector and stay on the computer. The Screen list leaves out delete, remove and sign-out buttons.

## Showdown Arena

A Kahoot-style team race, with no student devices. You ask a question out loud (or let the arena bring one). The team that answers well gets your tap, and on the projector its bar leaps, game-show stings play and a flame shows a streak of three. The Show mode opens on the **Team Forge**, where you set everything up before a single thing is saved.

**1 · Teams for this show.** Split the class six ways: **Fair** (even on this month's stars), **Mixed** (guilds mixed), **Luck**, **Guilds** (as they are, for this show only), **Today** (today's Team Maker teams) or **Dragon** (the whole class against the Dragon). Choose 2 to 6 teams and **Shuffle** (teammates from last time are kept apart). Tap a name to move a hero to the next team. Heroes marked away today are never put in a team; the Forge names who is away. Guild membership is never changed.

**2 · The game.**

| Game | How it plays |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Bars climb; the most points wins. Play until you finish, to a number of points (a finish line), or for a number of questions. |
| **Tug of War** | Two teams pull one rope. Every point moves the knot their way; get far enough ahead (3 to 10 points) and you win. |
| **Survivor** | Every team has 1 to 5 hearts. A wrong answer (**Miss**) costs one; a team with no hearts is out. The last team standing wins. If a miss would knock out every team left, they all keep their last heart. |
| **Treasure** | Every point opens a chest: a few coins, a double for the next point, or a steal from the leader. Teams behind find better chests. |

**3 · How it plays.** **Buzz in** (one team answers; their point ends the question) or **Every team** (whiteboards: tap each team that got it, then Next). An **answer clock** of 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds (or off), which can start by itself with every question: a gold ring, a heartbeat for the last seconds and a gong when time is up. A **streak bonus** (three in a row: +1), an **underdog boost** (a team three behind the leader earns +1) and a **hot seat** that gives every hero a turn at the microphone and crowns star players.

**4 · Questions.** **I ask** (as always), **Past quizzes** (questions from Quiz of the Week quizzes this class has already played, the ones it missed first; this week's quiz stays a secret until it is played), **Book words**, or both. For **Book words**, choose the book (any book with a wordlist), the units (earlier ones too; the unit the class has reached is marked; quick choices are **Latest 3**, **All so far** and **Whole book**) and the kinds of question: meanings (Greek both ways), definitions, gap-fills from the book's own sentences, spelling and missing letter. The question shows on the big screen; your Wand shows the right answer, for your eyes only, and can reveal it.

**While it runs**, the projector shows each hero's face under the team name, the hot-seat hero ringed. Your phone gives **+1 / +2 / +3**, **Several** (pick a few teams and give them the point together), **Golden question** (the next point counts double), **Undo** (also after the finish), **Pass the mic**, **Hide scores** for a big reveal, and **Next**. A hero marked away mid-show leaves the team and the hot seat at once (the team keeps its points). **Finish** builds a podium with confetti.

- **Reward (optional):** after the finish, **Teamwork stars** gives 1 to 3 ordinary **Teamwork** stars to the winners, everyone, or the star players. It follows the usual one-award-a-day rule, so heroes who already have today's stars (or are away) are skipped. The Showdown never creates a new kind of star or Gold. Then **Rematch** or **New teams**.
- **Nursery and Pre-Junior** (Growth Festival rules): no numbers, ranks or podium. Each team grows a flower with every good answer, and at the end the whole garden blooms. Only the race is played, and the reward gives everyone a Teamwork star.

## Quiz host console (Elite)

While Quiz of the Week runs, your phone shows the question, whose turn it is and **the correct answer, which only you can see**. When the hero says an answer, tap the matching colour. The projector reveals it exactly as if you had pressed the key. **Next**, **Skip** and **Listen** are there too.

## How this feeds the rest of the Quest

The Wand adds no rules of its own. Every star, crown, spin and reward goes through the same buttons and the same saving as the mouse. Prices, limits, birthdays, Hero-of-the-Day rules and plan gates all apply as usual. If something is not possible (for example, the hero already has today's stars), your phone vibrates and says why.

## Good to know

- **Weak classroom computers:** the effects get lighter on slower machines, and with **reduced motion** turned on they become a soft glow.
- Projector Mode's full-screen switch still needs a click on the computer (browsers only allow it from a real click there).
- A tap sent while your phone had no signal is dropped, never played minutes later.
- Your phone stays awake while the Wand is open, where the phone allows it.
- **Putting the Wand down:** press **Disconnect** (in the recent spells sheet under the wand crest), the ✕ at the top, or move to another projector, and the classroom computer lets go at once. If another phone takes the Wand, yours steps aside. If the connection drops, the phone tries again by itself.
- Only one phone holds a projector at a time. If you reload the classroom computer, or open the app in a second tab there, the Wand carries on without running every spell twice.
